# Meeting Prep Sheet

Preparation makes meetings more effective. Create and bring a hard copy "meeting prep sheet" to each scheduled meeting (for graduate students the meeting will not start without it). There is no required format, however, it must include the student's name, meeting date, and the following three sections.

## Update on action items from the last meeting
-	List all action items from the last meeting (plus any additional action items from between meetings) and their status.

## Agenda for today’s meeting
-	What do you want to talk about in our meeting today?
-	Good things to do in a meeting include making decisions, sharing information, seeking or providing feedback, and generating ideas.
-	A general status update should not be an item since the update in the previous section covers that.

## Action items for the next meeting
-	What items do you plan on completing before our next meeting?
-	Make the action items specific and realistic, but also make sure you are being proactive to achieve your longer-term goals.
